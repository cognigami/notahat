Let’s agree on a few conventions. In each post, I’ll give you a passage in
English, and the source French below. 

You’ll see words or phrases surrounded by `[`square brackets`]`. The outer brackets
signal that I had a choice to make between alternatives. The inner brackets
list the possibilities. I’ll interrupt<label for"sn-buthow" class="margin-toggle sidenote-number"></label>
<span class="sidenote">Around the English text and here in the margins</span>
to explain my thoughts and decisions about how I made the choice that
I settled on.


The French text comes from the 1943 Harcourt Brace printing. 

If you have a suggestion how I might do this better, please let me know.
